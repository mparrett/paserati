The shaded band means one of two things, and the legend says which. On the micro tabs it is how much the plotted benchmarks disagree with each other at that commit — not error bars, and it moves when the suite's composition changes independently of anything the engine did. On Test262 it is min..max across the three runs behind that point, which is real measurement uncertainty; the macro is measured three times per snapshot and the median plotted.
